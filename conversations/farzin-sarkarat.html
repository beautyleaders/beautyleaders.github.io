About this episode

Professor Farzin Sarkarat, Full Professor of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery and Adjunct Professor at Gulf Medical University in the United Arab Emirates, is the guest of this Beauty Leaders episode. Alongside more than two decades of specialist clinical work, he brings academic teaching experience, scientific research activity and an established conference record.

The conversation asks what should have the final word in aesthetic medicine. From anatomy and preservation of function in facial surgery to medical ethics, professional responsibility, patient choice and the importance of saying no to inappropriate requests, the discussion stays focused on responsible decision making.

It also addresses commercial influence on treatment decisions, scientific independence in physician collaboration with companies, the distinction between scientific education and product marketing, the risks of fillers and minimally invasive treatments, and the future of surgery in the age of artificial intelligence. One of the episode’s distinguishing elements is a clear exchange of differing views on professional ethics and the spread of non scientific behaviour in aesthetic medicine.

By making room for differing views on professional ethics, the conversation asks where technical possibility should give way to responsible clinical judgment.
