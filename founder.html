Iman Azarkhish

Founder and Host of Beauty Leaders, Biomedical Engineer and Entrepreneur

Iman Azarkhish is the founder and host of Beauty Leaders, an independent editorial media platform dedicated to meaningful conversations in aesthetic medicine.

With more than 15 years of experience in the medical aesthetics industry, his professional background encompasses biomedical engineering, medical technology, international business development, regulatory affairs, professional education and medical device manufacturing.

He is also the founder and CEO of Shafa Tajhiz Azarakhsh. His commercial activities are disclosed and remain separate from the editorial decisions of Beauty Leaders.
