01 · Experts

For physicians & experts

Beauty Leaders offers physicians and experts an independent editorial platform to share their scientific perspectives, professional achievements, clinical experience and personal journeys with a wider audience.

Each conversation is individually researched and developed around the guest’s expertise and experience. Interviews can take place in our studio or remotely, with the topic, language, format and timing agreed in advance.

02 · Institutions

For congresses & institutions

Beauty Leaders welcomes international media collaborations that extend the reach of scientific dialogue beyond congress venues and event dates.

Potential collaborations include interviews with international speakers, editorial coverage, conversations on emerging scientific topics and original editorial content developed before, during or after scientific events.

All collaborations are developed through mutual agreement, with respect for scientific accuracy, appropriate permissions and editorial independence.
