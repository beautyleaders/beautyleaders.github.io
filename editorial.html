Editorial trust

Editorial principles

Independence, accuracy and respect are the foundations of trust.

Policy draft for owner approval · 20 November 2026

  1. Editorial independence

    Guests, topics and questions are selected for relevance, knowledge and experience, not payment or follower counts. Guest appearances are not sold. Any future financial support must be transparent and must not control editorial decisions.

  2. Evidence and accuracy

    We distinguish scientific evidence, individual experience, professional opinion and promotion. Uncertainty should be acknowledged and important claims sourced where appropriate. Beauty Leaders is not a formal scientific review body.

  3. Respect and human dignity

    We avoid fear, humiliation, manufactured bodily insecurity and pressure to seek treatment. Informed choice includes the decision not to undergo an intervention.

  4. Disclosure of interests

    The founder is also CEO of Shafa Tajhiz Azarakhsh. Relevant interests should be disclosed in the content concerned. Conversations are not designed to promote products, brands or devices.

  5. Corrections and feedback

    Please send the page URL, the issue and a supporting source through our contact route. We aim to review and correct substantive errors, with the date and nature of material changes recorded.

  6. Medical information and endorsement

    Conversations do not replace individual medical advice. A guest appearance or opinion is not an endorsement of a treatment, product or professional credential by the media initiative.

  7. Partnerships

    Any actual collaboration will be described accurately, with appropriate permissions and disclosure. Editorial independence and accuracy remain essential.
